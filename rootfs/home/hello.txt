Welcome to DIOS!

DIOS is a minimalistic multi-tasking x86_64 operating system written in D language with some small parts in NASM assembly, plus a simple system environment also fully written in D. It is tested on a real hardware and emulators like VirtualBox and QEMU.

Many features of DIOS are inspired by Unix, but this is only to keep a degree of familiarity with existing systems for better user experience; making a full Unix clone is not the goal of the project. The main purpose is experimenting with the OS design and demonstrating D's fitness for system development.
